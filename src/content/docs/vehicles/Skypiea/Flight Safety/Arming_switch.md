To arm the aircraft, a special sequence has been used. This prevents accidental arms by making the switch quite delebrate. 

> Note that the aircraft can only be armed after ALL safety checks within Ardupilot have been cleared. Additionally, arming a plane should only be done with no individuals in the prop arc. For testing, propellers should ALWAYS be removed.

The arming switch on the TX16s is performed by:
 1. Hold `SH` switch up
 2. Move `SF` switch up
 3. Release `SH` switch

To Disarm, simply bring the `SF` switch down.
